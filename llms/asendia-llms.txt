# Asendia

> Asendia is a global provider of international e-commerce shipping and cross-border delivery. A joint venture between La Poste and Swiss Post, Asendia helps online retailers and brands ship parcels, manage returns, handle customs and fulfil orders across more than 200 destinations. Its e-PAQ range covers economy, tracked and premium delivery options, alongside customs clearance, global returns management and order fulfilment.

Asendia operates through several regional websites that represent the same company, including asendia.com (global), asendia.co.uk (United Kingdom), asendiausa.com (United States) and asendiaoceania.com (Oceania).
